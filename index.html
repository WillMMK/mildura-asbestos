Asbestos removal in Mildura and Sunraysia

Many homes, sheds and farm buildings around Mildura, Irymple, Red Cliffs and Merbein were built when asbestos cement sheeting was common. Fibro walls, eaves, old roofing, flues and vinyl floor tiles can all contain it. If you're renovating, demolishing a shed or have storm or fire damage, the material should be checked before anyone cuts, drills or breaks it.

In Victoria, asbestos removal is regulated by WorkSafe Victoria. Licensed removalists must follow set rules for removal, containment and disposal. Over the river in Buronga (NSW), SafeWork NSW rules apply, so the removalist must be licensed in NSW.

Asbestos testing

You can't tell if a material contains asbestos by looking at it. A sample can be taken and sent to an accredited laboratory for analysis. If you just want to know what you're dealing with before a renovation or sale, ask for testing or an inspection in the form below.

Asbestos disposal

Asbestos waste must be wrapped and taken only to a site licensed to accept it. Don't put it in household bins or skip bins. A licensed removalist can handle disposal as part of the job. Check with Mildura Rural City Council or EPA Victoria for current local disposal options.
